Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy describes general information practices for RK Digital World Smart Print. The data handled depends on the feature used and the hosting setup.

Files you process

Some tools may process files directly in your browser. Features that let you upload files for a print request may transmit files to the website server for processing or approval. Do not upload sensitive files unless you understand the feature and are comfortable using it.

Technical information

The hosting provider or server may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, request logs, and error information for operation, security, and troubleshooting.

Cookies and advertising

The website may use technical mechanisms needed to operate. If services such as Google AdSense are added, those services may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies and applicable consent requirements.

Third-party services

Hosting providers, external libraries, and other services may process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Security and retention

Reasonable steps are intended to protect the website and submitted files, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. File retention depends on the feature and server configuration.

Changes and questions

This policy may be updated as features change. Review it periodically. See Contact Us for contact options.
